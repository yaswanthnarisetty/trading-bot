# BTCUSD Delta Demo status and safety boundary

Legacy BTC execution is paused at the HTTP boundary. The legacy BTC session and local-position code cannot submit Delta orders. The separate daily Demo forward trial is explicitly enabled by `BTC_DEMO_AUTO_TRADING=ENABLED` in the local backend environment. It uses the Demo-only broker adapter; no production Delta endpoint is present. The BTC page shows both the experimental daily trial and the old read-only intraday preview.

The BTC page has a **Pause new Demo entries** control. Its authenticated setting is stored in MongoDB and survives backend restarts. Pausing does not cancel an already protected position or stop broker reconciliation and exits. Resume restores the daily entry scanner only while the backend Demo flag remains enabled.

On 2026-10-08, Delta Demo reported equity of $158.74967052, zero exchange BTC contracts, zero active BTC orders, and no recent BTC fills. Seven old local `OPEN` position records had no Delta order or fill identifiers. `node scripts/reconcileBtcLegacy.cjs --apply` marked them `UNVERIFIED_LEGACY` without changing their original `OPEN` statuses or inventing exits, and retired one old `RUNNING` session. The script refuses to apply if the exchange has a position, order, or recent fill. Run it without `--apply` to inspect current counts.

The new broker adapter targets only `https://cdn-ind.testnet.deltaex.org`. It signs each private request, requires a unique client order ID, sends entry orders with stop and target bracket fields, and uses reduce-only for explicit closes. A separate durable intent collection reserves a unique account slot before a POST. An uncertain POST is never retried automatically. Reconciliation requires the broker order, fills, and real-time position to agree; otherwise the intent remains in `ATTENTION` and blocks another entry. Startup and 15-second recovery passes only read broker state and update that evidence.

On 2026-10-08, the explicit one-contract broker smoke placed a **LONG 1 BTCUSD contract on Delta Demo**, then a reduce-only close. Broker entry order `2174895519` filled at $81,150; close order `2174895530` filled at $81,173.50. Net P&L after commissions was **−$0.07227087**, equal to the Demo equity change from $158.74967052 to $158.67739965. Broker positions and open orders returned to zero. The order API echoed the requested stop/target brackets; this test did not trigger either bracket. The initial local reconciliation flagged `ATTENTION` because it parsed the exchange's ISO fill timestamp as a number. The parser now accepts ISO and microsecond timestamps, rechecked both fill IDs and the flat position, and durably marked the intent `CLOSED_CONFIRMED`. That error is covered by the integration test. The smoke command is `node -r ts-node/register -r dotenv/config scripts/btcDemoSmoke.cjs --execute-demo-smoke` from `backend/`; it is limited to one contract, a 0.5% estimated risk budget and one completed smoke per 24 hours. It is not a strategy trading path.

The current EMA/RSI/volume strategy fails the cost-aware gate. The 2026-10-08 30-day replay used complete BTCUSD bars, a 70/30 chronological split, a 30 bp round-trip cost allowance, 1× notional cap, 0.5% estimated risk per trade, and a 2% daily stop. Held-out net P&L was approximately **−$30.34** at 1 minute, **−$23.76** at 5 minutes, and **−$9.38** at 15 minutes from a $158.75 starting balance. These are simulated results, not expected live returns. The replay omits actual order-book fills and funding, so a positive replay would still need more validation. Re-run with `BTC_RESEARCH_RESOLUTION=1m|5m|15m node -r ts-node/register -r dotenv/config scripts/btcResearch.cjs` from `backend/`.

The 9 October 2026 [strategy study](BTC_STRATEGY_RESEARCH.md) tested four fixed hypotheses and qualified none. The user's experimental daily Demo trial does **not** turn that failed research result into a profit claim. It evaluates one completed UTC daily candle, attempts entry only in the first five minutes of the next UTC day, and enforces a prior-day stop, 2R target, one entry per day, a three-loss/three-day pause, a five-day maximum hold, 0.5% estimated stop risk including a 30 bp cost allowance, 1× notional, fresh book/mark evidence, a 2% daily account stop and durable broker reconciliation. A skipped or unfillable setup sends no order. The Demo entry/exit plumbing and bracket-field echo were verified; actual stop/target triggering remains untested. If an intent shows `ATTENTION`, inspect the exchange position and orders before any further submission. Do not re-send a timed-out order or mark a local record closed from a price observation alone.

The 13 October [daily-candle study](BTC_DAILY_RESEARCH.md) found only 213 completed Demo bars; every observed training signal exceeded the one-contract 0.5% risk budget at the current Demo equity, and validation had no signals. Automatic Demo entry is an opt-in forward experiment rather than a validated deployment. At roughly $158.68 equity, a valid breakout may still be rejected by the one-contract risk minimum; do not increase risk or force a trade to bypass that rule.

Exchange API reference: <https://docs.delta.exchange/> (authentication, `GET /positions`, `GET /fills`, `POST /orders`, `GET /orders/client_order_id/{client_oid}`, and bracket/reduce-only fields).
